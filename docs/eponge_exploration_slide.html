What we audited, what the field knows, where we go next

What we audited

  • Seniors' pipeline: the "4% vs 10%" claim compares synthetic MAE with real parameter bounds; no classical baseline was ever run; synthetic pores are 2–4× coarser than real; Blender render and mask are not pixel-aligned; the reconstruction JSON reports 0.000 porosity error for every method.
  • Planning report: promises an SE(3)-equivariant 3D U-Net while scoping a slice-wise model; drifted to fuel-cell cathodes although the data is PEMWE anode.
  • Real data: three stacks on disk (comp 354, uncomp 410, pristine 665 slices) matching Ferner's three regions. Zero-knob Otsu lands within ~3 points of Ferner's hand-tuned value.
  • ChatGPT pseudo-masks: aligned, but a conservative threshold: half of Ferner's porosity, wrong region ordering, Pt cap labelled as catalyst in one stack.

Literature, ≈30 sources

  • Consensus since 2012 (Salzer, Prill, Moroni–Thiele, Osenberg 2023): segment from intensity evolution across neighbouring slices. Osenberg: milling-evolution features 6.1% error vs threshold 60.8%.
  • The seniors' idea was tried in 2021 by Fraunhofer ITWM; their 2024 retrospective calls the synthetic data "too clean" and the gap unsolved.
  • What worked (Sardhara 2022): physics-simulated shine-through, a 2D U-Net fed 3–9 adjacent slices (beat 3D nets on real data), label-free validation via xy-vs-z anisotropy.
  • Industry segments with painted labels (Dragonfly, Avizo, ilastik) or fills pores physically. No public IrO₂ CL benchmark, no open pipeline with uncertainty bars.
The gap is not a better architecture. It is validation: no one has shown a label-free method finds the cut face on unfilled IrO₂, and no one publishes the porosity with an error bar. That is the contribution we can own.

Proposed direction · Éponge v2

  1. Deterministic preprocessing: layer detection, de-curtaining, normalization. No user knobs.
  2. Classical baselines built in (Otsu, Ferner-style) with a pore / solid / uncertain consensus.
  3. Sparse hand labels on random patches with ±3-slice context, two annotators, agreement reported.
  4. 2.5D z-aware U-Net trained on depth-shaded synthetic data plus confident labels; sub-surface ablation.
  5. Ensemble uncertainty → porosity and tortuosity as median with 10–90 band. Cut: equivariant nets, mesh-method comparison.
