The real data behind the models. Three plasma-FIB-SEM stacks of IrO₂ anode catalyst layers, studied by Ferner et al. (2024): compressed under a PTL fibre (comp), above a PTL pore (uncomp) and never assembled (pristine). Each has 116–118 slices labelled by four annotators in two rounds: everyone annotated their own slices plus 12 shared slices, then each annotator's slices were cross-checked by another. How the labels were made
1 · Start masksBoundary cleaned slice-to-slice; pore vs solid decided with a 7-slice shine-through rule (a reimplementation of Ferner et al.'s z-gradient method).
2 · Round 1: annotateFour annotators each corrected ~26 own slices by hand, flipping ±3 slices to tell real cut faces from material seen through pores, plus 12 shared slices done independently.
3 · Round 2: cross-checkEvery annotator's slices were reviewed by another; each change was logged.
4 · MergeShared slices: 4-way majority vote. Other slices: the owner's version after cross-check.

Fleiss' kappa on the 12 slices all four labelled independently (1 = identical, > 0.8 is "almost perfect"). Annotators started from the same masks and changed under 1 % of pixels, so most agreement is on untouched pixels.

Mask values in the dataset files: 0 = pore, 255 = solid, 128 = outside. Porosity = pore / (pore + solid). Images here are downsized to 900 px wide for the browser.
